# PTR.AINEWS Editorial & Verification Standard

## Source hierarchy
1. Primary and official sources: official documentation, company newsrooms, standards bodies, regulators, original research.
2. Independent confirmation where a material claim warrants it.
3. PTR explanation and analysis, explicitly separated from sourced facts.
4. Social/community material may be a discovery lead, not sole proof for a material factual claim.

## Article structure
- Content label
- Headline
- Published date / Updated date
- What happened
- What the primary source says
- PTR explanation or analysis
- How we verified this
- Sources
- Material uncertainty/correction note when applicable

## Labels
- VERIFIED — factual item checked against adequate source evidence.
- EXPLAINER — PTR explains sourced information in plain language.
- ANALYSIS — interpretation; must not be presented as sourced fact.
- GUIDE — practical educational material.
- DEVELOPING — facts may materially change; clearly state uncertainty.

## Bilingual rule
English factual master is verified first. Khmer translation must preserve factual meaning, labels, dates, uncertainty and the same source set. Translation must not introduce new factual claims.

## Corrections
Material corrections should be disclosed and dated. Do not silently alter a claim in a way that hides a meaningful prior error.

## Commercial separation
Advertising and sponsored content must be clearly labeled and must not influence VERIFIED status or source conclusions.

## AI use
AI-generated wording is not itself a source. Every material factual claim still requires adequate source evidence.
