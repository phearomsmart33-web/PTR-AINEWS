Google introduces Gemini 3.8 Live and Extended Thinking

Published by PTR.AINEWS · 26 Sep 2026 · Source checked 26 Sep 2026

What happened

Google announced Gemini 3.8 Live and Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking on September 15, 2026, with an update dated September 17.

What the primary source says

Google describes Gemini 3.8 Live as optimized for conversational intelligence, fluid dialogue and visual grounding, while Extended Thinking is intended for higher-complexity, multi-step tasks.

PTR explanation

Live multimodal models can matter for customer service and operational workflows where users need voice, visual context and tool execution in one interaction. Real deployment still requires testing latency, cost, accuracy and privacy for the specific workflow.
