Google expands its AI & Economy ATLAS with new global AI-use insights

Published by PTR.AINEWS · 26 Sep 2026 · Source checked 26 Sep 2026

What happened

Google published new AI & Economy ATLAS data and an open-access interactive experience on September 15, 2026. Google says the project examines how AI is being used across work, science and creative activity.

What the primary source says

Among examples highlighted by Google, arts, design and media occupations account for 19% of work-related AI usage in India, while computer and mathematical occupations account for 30% of work-related AI usage in the United States.

PTR explanation

The useful point is not that one country has “won” AI adoption. The data instead illustrates that AI use can differ substantially by occupation and market. Businesses should evaluate adoption at the workflow level rather than relying only on broad global AI trends.

How we verified this

Primary source: Google, “New insights from Google’s AI & Economy ATLAS,” Sep. 15, 2026. Last checked: Sep. 26, 2026.
