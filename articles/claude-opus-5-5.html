Anthropic introduces Claude Opus 5.5

PTR.AINEWS · 26 Sep 2026 · Source checked 26 Sep 2026

What happened

Anthropic announced Claude Opus 5.5 on September 22, 2026 as the first model in its Claude 5.5 family.

What the primary source says

Anthropic says Opus 5.5 performs at the level of Claude Fable 5.1 on most work while costing 40% less to run than Opus 5. The company also says the model underwent external evaluation before release.

PTR explanation

For organizations, model selection should consider workload quality, price, safeguards and integration requirements together. Vendor-reported performance claims should be evaluated against a team’s own representative tasks before production adoption.
